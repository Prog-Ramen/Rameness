CONTROLS

WASDMove
MouseLook around
Left clickMine a block (hold)
Right clickPlace the selected block
Middle clickPick the block you are looking at
SpaceJump / fly up
ShiftSneak, or fly down
CtrlSprint (or double-tap W)
1–9Select hotbar slot
WheelCycle hotbar
FToggle flying
TAdvance time to the next phase
RRespawn on the surface
F3Debug overlay
EscPause menu

BLOCK PICKER

Click a block to put it in hotbar slot 1. Press E or Esc to close.
