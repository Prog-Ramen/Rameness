Voxelcraft

An infinite voxel world that runs in your browser — no build step, no downloads.

Click the world to capture the mouse. Esc releases it.

Paused

Settings

Caves and trees apply to newly generated chunks.

Controls

W A S DMove
SpaceJump / fly up / swim
ShiftSneak (crouch, will not walk off edges)
Ctrl / WSprint
ECreative flight (double-tap Space too)
Left clickBreak block (hold to keep mining)
Right clickPlace block from hotbar
Middle clickPick block into hotbar
1…9 / wheelSelect hotbar slot
TCycle held block
FToggle third person
F1Save screenshot (PNG)
F3Toggle debug panel
CToggle creative mode (instant break)
EscPause / release mouse
