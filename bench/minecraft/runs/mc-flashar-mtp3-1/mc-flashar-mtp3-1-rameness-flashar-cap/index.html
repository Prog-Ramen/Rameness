Voxelcraft

A block world in your browser. Dig, build, explore.

Controls

W A S D
walk (arrow keys work too)
Mouse
look around — click the world to capture the mouse; if your browser refuses that, drag instead
Left click / hold
break the block under the crosshair
Right click / hold
place the held block
Middle click
pick the block you are looking at
1 … 9, mouse wheel
choose a block
Space
jump, swim up, fly up (when flying)
C or Ctrl
descend (when flying)
Shift
sprint
F
toggle flying
T
advance the time of day
R
level the view
G or F3
show or hide the debug readout
M
sound on or off
P
save
Page Up / Page Down
view distance
Tab
show or hide this list
Esc
menu

Blocks are one metre each. Bedrock cannot be broken. Your building is saved in this browser, so coming back later restores the world you left.
