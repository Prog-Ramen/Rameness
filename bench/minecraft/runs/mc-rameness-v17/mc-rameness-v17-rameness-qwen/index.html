an infinite Minecraft-ish world, generated right in your browser

W A S D move  ·  Mouse look
Space jump / swim  ·  Ctrl sprint  ·  Shift sneak
Left click break block  ·  Right click place block
1–9 / wheel select block  ·  F or double-Space fly  ·  R respawn
