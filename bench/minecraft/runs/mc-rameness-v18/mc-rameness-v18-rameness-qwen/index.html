Voxelcraft

A tiny Minecraft clone that runs entirely in your browser — no install, no build step.

  • Click the world to capture the mouse and start playing
  • W A S D move  ·  Mouse look
  • Space jump / swim  ·  Ctrl sprint (fly fast)  ·  Shift sneak (fly down)
  • F toggle fly mode
  • Left click break block  ·  Right click place block
  • 1–9, 0 / wheel pick block from hotbar
  • Esc release the mouse

Your edits are saved in this browser (localStorage) and survive a reload. The world is generated procedurally — caves, water, sand, and trees included.
