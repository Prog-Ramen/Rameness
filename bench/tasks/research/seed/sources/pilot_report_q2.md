# Northwind pilot — Q2 2026 (Reno site)
We ran Helix R3 and Kestrel K7 units side by side for six weeks. Otter O2 was not available for the pilot.

| | Helix R3 | Kestrel K7 |
|---|---|---|
| Measured throughput (picks/hour, 6-week average) | 430 | 455 |
| Measured battery life (hours) | 8.7 | 6.8 |
| Unplanned downtime | 1.2% | 3.9% |

Note: Kestrel K7 fell short of its datasheet throughput in every week of the pilot.
