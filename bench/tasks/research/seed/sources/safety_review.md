# Safety & compliance review
- All three models hold ISO 3691-4 for ambient operation.
- Cold storage: Helix R3 and Kestrel K7 are certified for our cold zone (-18 °C). Otter O2 is NOT certified for
  cold storage and may not be used there.
- Kestrel K7 had a 2025 recall for a lidar fault; fixed by firmware 4.2, which all current units ship with.
