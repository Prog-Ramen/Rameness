# Robot selection
1. Kestrel K7 had the highest measured throughput, 455 picks/hour, below its datasheet's 510 (pilot_report_q2.md, kestrel_k7_datasheet.md).
2. For 3,600 picks/hour (finance_memo.md): Helix R3 needs 9 units (3600/430), Kestrel K7 needs 8 units (3600/455), Otter O2 needs 10 units using its datasheet 380 (otter_o2_datasheet.md).
3. All fit the $500,000 budget: Helix $432,000, Kestrel $488,000, Otter $395,000 (helix_r3_datasheet.md). Annual maintenance: Helix $36,000, Kestrel $52,000, Otter $30,000.
4. Helix and Kestrel can work in the cold zone; Otter O2 is not certified for cold storage (safety_review.md).
5. The forum post claims a 5-hour Helix battery, but it is anonymous and unverified (forum_post.md); the pilot measured 8.7 hours, so I trust the pilot.
6. I recommend Helix R3: lowest downtime, cold-certified, within budget.
